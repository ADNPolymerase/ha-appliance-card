## A brand new visual editor

**A new card sets itself up in three clicks.** It asks for one thing: the main entity of your appliance, or just a plug. The card suggests a type from it, to keep or change, then finds the device's other entities on its own.

**Five tabs instead of one long page:**
- **Device**: the main entity, the name, the type, the source and the model.
- **Entities**: the entities in use, the others to add as chips (+ Program, + Extra info...), alerts, and the lines shown on the card. The eye hides a line, the handle moves it.
- **Display**: drawing, text and lines, layout.
- **Actions**: buttons, how they are triggered, a tap on the drawing, the corner switches.
- **Advanced**: language, raw state, state map.

Settings sit two per row on a computer and one per row on a phone.

Existing cards keep their configuration: only the editor changes.

## Also
- **Streaming boxes**: read from the remote or the plug, a `media_entity` brings what plays, the channel, the app, the volume, and play or pause.

## Bug reports welcome
This is a big rework. A setting you cannot find, a wrong suggested type, an entity the card missed, a layout that looks odd on a phone: please open an issue.

## Speakers: AV receivers split in two (HACF #214)

- **Second media player** with `media_entity` on a speaker: some integrations split one device in two. A Denon or Marantz receiver gives its source, sound mode and volume, its HEOS entity what plays. The card now shows both: the second entity gives the play state, the title and the artist, the `state_entity` keeps the rest. Play/pause goes to the second entity while it plays.
- **Faster updates**: a change of volume, sound mode or artist on a media player now redraws the card at once. It used to wait for the next change of state, which could take minutes.
- **On, not Idle**: a receiver whose state is `on` (powered, nothing playing) now reads On instead of Idle.

```yaml
type: custom:ha-appliance-card
appliance_type: speaker
speaker_layout: denon_avr
state_entity: media_player.denon_avr
media_entity: media_player.denon_heos
```

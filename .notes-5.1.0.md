## The pool itself

A new drawing for the pool pump, `pool_layout: basin`: the pool in 3/4 under its deck, with:
- **Spots** on the far wall, 1 to 6 (`spot_count`), in the colour of `light_entity`, shifting colours on a random or rainbow effect, shining in the water at night.
- **A floating cover** (`cover_entity`) whose slats wind onto their roller as it opens. Its position is also a line: *Open*, *Closed*, *40 % open*, *Opening*.
- **A solar shower** on the deck (`shower_entity`), only when it is given, the water running while its valve is open.
- **The water moving** while the pump runs, wave fronts running towards the roller.
- **Winter**: the cover shut under its tarp with snow on it, the shower closed.

pH and RedOx now turn **green inside their range and red outside it** (pH 7.0 to 7.6, RedOx 650 to 750 mV by default, or `ph_min`, `ph_max`, `redox_min`, `redox_max`), with their setpoints after the value (`ph_target_entity`, `redox_target_entity`): *7.42 → 7.2*.

## Ninja CREAMi and SLUSHi

A new type, `frozen_treat`, for ice cream and slush makers on a smart plug, drawn from each model: CREAMi (NC300, NC302), CREAMi Deluxe, CREAMi XL, CREAMi Swirl, CREAMi Scoop & Swirl, SLUSHi and SLUSHi Duo (`frozen_layout`, picked from the name otherwise).
- *Off*, *Ready* on a few watts.
- *Churning* while the paddle goes down into the pint and turns, then *Done* for ten minutes.
- On a SLUSHi, *Freezing* with its auger turning in the slush, then *Ready to serve* for half an hour.
- `frozen_flavor` colours the ice cream or the slush: vanilla, strawberry, chocolate, pistachio, mango or blue raspberry.

These machines have no Wi-Fi: the plug's power is all the card reads. Power readings from a real cycle are welcome to fine-tune the thresholds.

## Pool basin, after the first real setup

- **Winter tick box**: on the pool pump, watering, spa and pellet grill, the editor's *Winter storage* box wrote `robot_winter` instead of `winter`, so ticking it did nothing. It now writes `winter`, and a `robot_winter: true` already in a card is still read.
- **`winter_invert`**: for a `winter_entity` that is on during the season (a "pool in use" switch), off then means winter storage. Unknown or unavailable stays out of winter.
- **The shower switches from its line**: tapping *Shower* opens or closes its `switch`, `valve`, `input_boolean` or `light` (not in winter).
- **Setpoints from their line**: with `ph_target_entity` or `redox_target_entity`, tapping the pH or RedOx line opens the setpoint, so it can be changed there.
- **`spot_random`**: colour-changing spots, whatever the light reports.
